Stavish Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

The Stavish team (the "Operator") handles information about users of the chat app Stavish (the "Service") as follows.

1. What we collect and why

  • Email address and password: to let you log in and to confirm it's you. Passwords are stored encrypted; the Operator cannot read them.
  • A device identifier: to allow one account per smartphone and to stop suspended users from creating new accounts. It is stored only as a one-way hash and is never shown to other users.
  • Photos you post and their captions: to show them to you and your friends. They are kept in private storage and deleted after 7 days, or earlier when you delete them or your account. Reported posts are viewed by the Operator for review.
  • A record of who you met: to show how many people you have met, and their icons (“Met”), to you and your friends. We keep who the other person was, their country, when and how long you talked, and whether you became friends — never what was said. Met shows only icons, never names. In the same way, your icon may appear in the Met of people you met and of their friends. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Hobbies and Hobby Time use: to connect you with people who share a hobby, and to keep Hobby Time to three times in 24 hours. You can change or remove your hobbies on your profile.
  • Reactions, replies, announcements and favourite friends: to show your chats and friend list. Reactions, replies and announcements are shown to the people in the chat and deleted with it. Favourites (pins) are shown only to you.
  • Tries to send blocked words: to run the SV gauge. Only the time of each try is kept, never the message.
  • Likes: to show who liked a post, and how many, to people who can see that post.
  • A notification address (push token): to tell you about matches, messages and new friends. It is sent, with the notification text, through Expo's push notification service. Deleted when you log out or delete your account.
  • Year of birth: to keep out people under 13 and to connect people under 18 only with each other. It is never shown to other users.
  • Nickname and icon: shown to the people you chat with. Your icon is the first letter of your nickname or a photo you choose. Photos are stored in a small, reduced size and deleted when you change your icon or delete your account.
  • Your phone's language and region settings: to choose the translation language, to show your country to others, and for the "My country" option. We do not collect location (GPS).
  • Messages: to deliver your chats. They are deleted when the chat ends; chats with friends are kept until either of you unfriends, blocks, or deletes their account. Only reported chats are kept, for up to 30 days, for review.
  • Reports: to run the SV gauge and decide on suspensions.

2. Services we share information with

  • Supabase (login, database, server processing): all of the information above.
  • Translation services (MyMemory / Translated srl; depending on settings, Google Cloud Translation or DeepL): message text, for automatic translation.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): your push token and notification text, to deliver notifications.
  • Gmail (Google): your email address, to send confirmation and other emails.

If the Service shows ads, the ad service (Google AdMob) may collect information about your device and the app (such as your IP address) in order to show them. Ads are not based on your interests (non-personalized ads), and we never share your chats, posts or profile for advertising. We do not share personal information with any other third party except as required by law.

3. How long we keep it

  • Messages: until the chat ends (friend chats: until you stop being friends; reported chats: up to 30 days)
  • Account information: until you delete your account
  • Tries to send blocked words: 30 days
  • Device identifier (hash): kept after account deletion, so that each smartphone can have only one account

4. Deletion and access requests

You can delete your account at any time from the profile screen in the app. For requests to access your information, please contact us below.

5. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page.
