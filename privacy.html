FATE Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Month Day, Year] (fill in the date and the operator's name before publishing)

[Operator name] (the "Operator") handles information about users of the chat app FATE (the "Service") as follows.

1. What we collect and why

  • Email address and password: to let you log in and to confirm it's you. Passwords are stored encrypted; the Operator cannot read them.
  • A device identifier: to allow one account per smartphone and to stop suspended users from creating new accounts. It is stored only as a one-way hash and is never shown to other users.
  • Nickname and icon: shown to the people you chat with. Icons are chosen from a set provided in the app.
  • Your phone's language and region settings: to choose the translation language, to show your country to others, and for the "My country" option. We do not collect location (GPS).
  • Messages: to deliver your chats. They are deleted when the chat ends. Only reported chats are kept, for up to 30 days, for review.
  • Reports: to run the report gauge and decide on suspensions.

2. Services we share information with

  • Supabase (login, database, server processing): all of the information above.
  • Translation services (MyMemory / Translated srl; depending on settings, Google Cloud Translation or DeepL): message text, for automatic translation.
  • Gmail (Google): your email address, to send confirmation and other emails.

We do not share personal information with any other third party except as required by law, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How long we keep it

  • Messages: until the chat ends (reported chats: up to 30 days)
  • Account information: until you delete your account
  • Device identifier (hash): kept after account deletion, so that each smartphone can have only one account

4. Deletion and access requests

You can delete your account at any time from the profile screen in the app. For requests to access your information, please contact us below.

5. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page.
